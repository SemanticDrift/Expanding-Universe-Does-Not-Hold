## What This Does

Four converging lines of evidence show the universe is not expanding. Constellations and deep-field structures show no angular drift across three millennia. Hubble's redshift-distance relationship is real, but the velocity interpretation was never validated. Cosmological redshift is a visibility threshold effect from f = c/r, not a recession signal. Dark matter and dark energy percentages are model-fitted parameters, not independent measurements.

## The Core Insight

The geometric record of three thousand years, from Babylonian cuneiform tablets to the Gaia mission's catalogue of over one billion stars, shows a universe whose angular geometry is stable at every measurable scale. The Coma Cluster, the Virgo Supercluster, the Bullet Cluster, and JWST ultra-deep fields all show stable geometry in regions the standard model designates as expansion-dominated.

Hubble made the best call available in 1929. The data he lacked is now in hand. It does not confirm the velocity interpretation. The curvature-frequency identity f = c/r explains cosmological redshift as perimeter attenuation at the edge of any detector's visibility window.

The universe is not expanding. Its coordinate grid is fixed. Its redshift is a threshold effect. Its dark constituents are frequency bands outside our current detection window.
